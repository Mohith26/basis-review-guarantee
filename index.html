How the certificate works

  1. Score. A logistic risk score combines the agent's confidence with ledger history (prior codings for the vendor, firm coding rules, vendor ambiguity, amount vs. the vendor's norm). It is fit on April to June.
  2. Test, strict to loose. Cutoffs are fixed in advance from April to June. For each, from strictest to loosest, Basis tests "more than the target share of what this cutoff posts is miscoded" on July to September with an exact binomial p-value, and stops at the first cutoff it cannot reject (Learn-then-Test, fixed-sequence). The loosest rejected cutoff is certified: with the chosen confidence, the miscode rate among future transactions it posts is at most the target, provided October looks like the summer.
  3. Keep it honest. A random audit sample of posted items is still reviewed. For every feed, a beta-binomial mixture martingale tests "rate ≤ target" continuously; it can be checked every day without inflating false alarms. When it crosses 1/0.05 the feed stops posting and that month's posted items go back to review.
